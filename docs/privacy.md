# Dawnwell Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 26, 2026*

Dawnwell is a daily companion for people taking a GLP-1 medicine as a tablet. This policy explains what the app stores and where. The short version: **your data stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account. We do not run a server, and we cannot see your data.**

## What Dawnwell stores

You enter everything yourself. Depending on what you use, the app stores:

- your medication name, dose and usual time, copied from your prescription label;
- when you took each tablet and how long you waited before eating;
- food, protein, water, fiber and strength entries;
- symptoms, their severity and notes;
- weight entries and your weight and protein goals;
- your reminder settings.

## Where it is stored

- **On your iPhone.** All data is saved on the device.
- **In your own iCloud.** If you are signed in to iCloud, the data is synced to your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit. Only your Apple ID can read it. Dawnwell's developer has no access to it.
- **Copies you make.** The app keeps a few daily copies on your phone, and you can export a backup file. Where that file goes is up to you.

## Apple Health

If you allow it, Dawnwell reads your body weight from Apple Health to show it on your progress chart. Dawnwell only reads weight; it does not write to Apple Health. Readings from Apple Health are shown on screen but never saved by Dawnwell, so they are not copied to iCloud or into backup files. Health data is never used for advertising and never shared with anyone. You can turn off access any time in the Health app.

## Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which parts of the app help people, Dawnwell counts a few anonymous events, such as "finished setup", "logged a tablet", "opened Plus" or "bought Plus" with the plan name. They are sent to Google Analytics for Firebase. Along with the events, Firebase receives a random install ID, the app version, iOS version and device model, and a rough location (country or region) worked out from the IP address. There is no name, email, Apple ID or advertising identifier, ad signals are turned off, and the events are not linked to you or used for tracking. Google's handling of this data is described at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Nothing you log is ever in these events: no medication, dose, tablet times, food, protein, symptoms, weight or notes, and nothing from Apple Health.

You can turn this off at any time in the app under **Your data → Share anonymous usage**.

## What we do not do

- No account, no sign-up, no email collection.
- No tracking or advertising tools, and no data sold or shared for advertising.
- No Dawnwell server. The app does not send your logs to us.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server.

## Purchases

Dawnwell Plus subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is active.

## Sharing your report

When you share a visit report or PDF, you choose who receives it, for example by Mail or Messages. Dawnwell does not send it anywhere on its own.

## Deleting your data

- Delete individual entries in the app.
- Deleting the app removes the data from your iPhone.
- To remove the iCloud copy, open **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Dawnwell** and delete its data.

## Not medical advice

Dawnwell records and reminds. It never suggests or changes a dose. Always follow your prescriber's instructions.

## Children

Dawnwell is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
